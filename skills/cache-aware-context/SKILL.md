# Cache Aware Context

Keep stable instructions and tool schemas; do not mutate past messages just to chase cache hits. Load skills because they are applicable or explicitly required, and reuse unchanged loaded content. Use bounded reads and recoverable excerpts. Measure actual cached/input fields with privacy-preserving task/run correlation. Do not claim cache sharing, permanent retention or billing savings from fingerprint equality.

Use only existing authorized provider lanes and native quotas. No paid fallback, allowance probes or model-backed polling. Preserve owner decisions, rights, review and publication gates. Do not interrupt active workers or modify unrelated services. Report meaningful completion, failure or a material owner question; stay quiet on unchanged waits. Role guidance confers no additional tool or filesystem permission.
