# Quota and provider policy

- Inherit existing native profile/host quotas, cooldowns, concurrency caps and retry limits.
- Use only routes the operator has already authorized. No shared credentials are included.
- Disable paid fallback. An unavailable permitted lane becomes a wait, a bounded alternative within existing authorization, or a material owner question.
- Do not make model calls to poll a process, probe allowance or manufacture acceptance evidence.
- Keep manual suggestions manual. A route-selection policy must not introduce inference on typing, idle, mount or reload.
- A cached-token count does not prove shared provider cache origin, unlimited cache retention or a particular monetary saving.
- Confirm modality and provider terms using the operator's actual route. A Web or subscription lane is not automatically free or eligible for every use.
